Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how ChatPhantom collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our website and services.

1. Who We Are

ChatPhantom is a project operated by an individual based in the United Kingdom. For privacy-related inquiries, you can contact us at chatphantomreceive@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in using Google, we collect your email address and profile picture.
  • Chat Analysis: When you upload a chat for analysis while signed in, we store the processed output of the analysis, which is linked to your account.
  • Gemini API: To generate insights, your chat content is securely sent to Google’s Gemini API. We store the output for your access via your dashboard.
  • Transactions: If you purchase credits, we store your transaction ID and link it to your account. Payment details are handled directly by Stripe or PayPal.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account via Google
  • To analyze your WhatsApp chat logs using AI
  • To display and manage your analysis history in your dashboard
  • To track purchases and manage your available credits

4. Third Parties

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Google: For user authentication (Google Sign-In) and chat analysis (Gemini API)
  • Stripe and PayPal: For processing payments securely
  • Firebase: For storing user accounts, analysis data, and transactions

5. Data Retention

We retain your analysis data and transaction records as long as your account is active. Users currently cannot delete their accounts, but this feature is planned for future updates. You may contact us directly to request deletion of specific analysis results.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office)

7. International Users

ChatPhantom primarily serves users in the UK, US, and other English-speaking regions. If you are accessing the site from outside these areas, your data may be processed in jurisdictions that may not offer the same level of data protection.

8. Security

We use secure technologies and services such as Firebase and encrypted connections to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or requests regarding your data, contact us at chatphantomreceive@gmail.com.
